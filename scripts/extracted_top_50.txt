Left out, and why
  "Near me" keywords: Google answers these from the Google Business Profile and the searcher's location,
so page text doesn't help.
 Seasonal keywords (January registration, year-end, February launch): better as a later blog calendar.
 Russian, Armenian and French-script keywords: only worthwhile once translated pages exist.
 "business registration office Saskatoon": likely brings people looking for the government office.
 T2 and corporate tax phrasings: corporate tax is positioned inside the bookkeeping relationship.
 Most branded variants: one is enough while the brand naming question is open.
Before finalizing

versions.

 "Cost" keywords can use public government fees; decide whether pages will show SaskStartupSolutions
pricing.
 Content for "how long does incorporation take" must not promise timelines.
 Confirm the website domain in the Brand & Marketing Facts file (it currently lists viciconsulting.ca).
