# Saskatchewan Corporate Registry Essentials: How to Protect Your Corporation from Involuntary Strike-Off

Maintaining an active corporation in Saskatchewan requires continuous compliance with Information Services Corporation (ISC), the official administrator of the Saskatchewan Corporate Registry. While federal filings like the T2 tax return satisfy CRA requirements, provincial compliance is governed entirely by ISC.

---

## 1. The Mandatory ISC Annual Return
Every Saskatchewan business corporation must file an **Annual Return** with ISC:
* **Filing Window:** The return is due on the last day of the month following your corporate anniversary month. For example, if your company was incorporated on June 15, your annual return is due by July 31 every year.
* **Consequences of Missing Deadlines:** Failure to file your annual return will lead to late fees, registry warnings, and eventually an involuntary corporate strike-off, rendering your company dissolved and unable to legally enter contracts or hold bank accounts.

---

## 2. Maintaining Director & Address Disclosures
Any significant change to your corporation must be promptly reported to ISC:
* Change of registered office address.
* Addition or resignation of corporate directors or officers.
* Share structure modifications or shareholder register updates.
