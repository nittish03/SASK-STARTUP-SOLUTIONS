# The Saskatchewan Founder's Quick Guide to T2 Corporate Tax Deadlines and Penalty Avoidance

If you run an incorporated business in Saskatoon or anywhere across Saskatchewan, understanding your corporate tax obligations is essential to avoiding costly penalties from the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).

Here is a straightforward summary of the key deadlines and rules every founder needs to know:

---

### The Two Critical Dates for Corporate Tax

Many new business owners make the mistake of confusing their **filing deadline** with their **payment deadline**:

1. **The Filing Deadline (6 Months Post Fiscal Year-End):**
   Your corporation must prepare and electronically file its T2 Corporation Income Tax Return within **6 months** after your corporate fiscal year ends. Even if your business generated zero revenue or operated at a loss, filing is mandatory.
   
2. **The Balance Owing Deadline (2 or 3 Months Post Fiscal Year-End):**
   If your corporation owes corporate tax, that balance must be remitted to the CRA **2 months** after your fiscal year-end (or **3 months** if your company qualifies as an eligible Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation claiming the Small Business Deduction).

### Why Organized Monthly Bookkeeping is Your Best Defense

Attempting to piece together an entire year’s worth of financial records at tax time is one of the biggest causes of delayed filings, lost deductions, and late penalties. 

Maintaining audit-ready books throughout the year ensures that:
* Every business expense and receipt is properly categorized under CRA rules.
* General Index of Financial Information (GIFI) codes align seamlessly with your financial statements.
* You never miss statutory tax deadlines.
